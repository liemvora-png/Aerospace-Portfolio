About me

Two tracks, one way of working

Music taught me to build a group that rehearses, performs and gets paid. Engineering taught me to build the tools that make it possible.

Who I am / background

Strings and servers

Two halves, and the habits that connect them.

I arrange, compose and conduct for strings — and I build the systems behind the work.

I founded a string ensemble of six people that performs weddings and private events, generating over $10,000 in revenue each year. I founded a school AI club that grew past 40 members, running hands-on sessions on applied machine learning. Underneath both sits a personal AI and cloud storage data server that I designed, built and maintain myself.

Conducting taught me that a group is a system: everyone knows their part, and someone has to keep the time. Building the server taught me the same thing about an idea.
