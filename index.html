Music / orchestral

Orchestral arrangement, composition, and conducting

Writing for strings, rehearsing them, and running the ensemble that books them.

Six strings, one ensemble

I started the ensemble with six players and grew it into a working group that takes wedding and private-event bookings. I write the charts, arrange for the lineup we have, and conduct from the front — which means every gig is also a rehearsal.

The same year I founded it, I founded a school AI club that passed 40 members, so the ensemble and the club ran in parallel. Keeping both on track is what pushed me to build my own AI and cloud storage server rather than juggling five different apps.

A good ensemble is a system: everyone knows their part, and someone has to keep the time.
