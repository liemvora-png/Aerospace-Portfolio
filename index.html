CONFIGURATION / A

Two-seat mission.

The proposed mission combines four hours of flight, 80 mph cruise, a 0.5-mile takeoff distance, and 300 lb for two passengers. Whether one aircraft can meet these goals with the listed engine, fuel, structure, and controls is not established.

03 / FLOW STUDY↗

Aerodynamics

Proposed layout: flaps, no ailerons, and vertical plus horizontal tail surfaces. Flaps do not provide the same function as ailerons; a suitable, validated lateral-control system is an unresolved requirement. Wing geometry, stall behavior, and takeoff distance are also unverified.

04 / POWERTRAIN STUDY↗

Propulsion

Proposed component: Polini Thor 250, 5,000 RPM target, two-blade propeller. The manufacturer lists a 36 hp maximum and 8,000 RPM maximum; it does not establish output or fuel consumption for this proposed aircraft operating point.

03 / Performance
