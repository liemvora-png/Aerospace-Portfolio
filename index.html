01 / Concept

A better connection
starts between cities.

Regional journeys are often too long for the road and too short for the airport. SKYWARD explores a purpose-built aircraft for that in-between distance—designed around flexibility, efficiency, and a lighter footprint.

DESIGN INTENTConnect communities

Explore practical regional links beyond major hubs.

DESIGN APPROACHElectric-first study

Investigate distributed propulsion at concept level.

PROJECT PHASEFeasibility & layout

Early design exploration; no aircraft has been built.
