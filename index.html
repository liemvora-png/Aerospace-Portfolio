01 — About

Two disciplines,
one working method.

I keep two things running at once: a string ensemble that books weddings, and an engineering practice that ranges from a personal AI and cloud storage server to a full aircraft design.

The two overlap more than you would expect. Arranging for six musicians is constraint solving — every voice has a range, every section has a count, and the arrangement has to work for the people actually holding the instruments. An ensemble only functions if everyone knows the plan, which is the same requirement as a working system.

The other half is that I would rather own the whole pipeline. I arrange the music, I run the bookings, I keep the books, I write the server the club runs on, and I spec the aircraft down to the welded aluminium. Each of those started as a problem I did not want to hand off.

03 — Music

Arranging, conducting, and the long way around a record.

Orchestral arrangement, composition & conducting

I arrange, compose, and conduct orchestral music — writing parts that fit the players I actually have, then standing in front of them and making the arrangement hold together in real time.

TMEA Region 23 All-State Qualifier. The ensemble is my own rehearsal room: a working group, a real repertoire, and a real audience every weekend.

The ensemble is where the engineering shows up. Six people, a tight rehearsal window, and a set list that has to work — that is a scheduling and systems problem wearing a bow tie.

Miles Davis

Milestones

“Straight No Chaser”

Red Garland on piano, a style “like Laufey mixed with Charlie Brown — simple, calm and nostalgic.” Garland had been fired by Davis and returned for the album; his own solo record Red Alone carries the same mood across a full set.

Mary Lou Williams

Free Spirits

“All Blues”

Drummer Mickey Roker, who later played on The Ellington Album — Ellington’s music assembled without Ellington, rebuilt from remixes. Roker could not read sheet music until late in his career and had memorised all of it.

Duke Ellington

Money Jungle

“Switch Blade”

Charlie Mingus on double bass — composer, bandleader, and the “angry man of jazz” behind The Black Saint and the Sinner Lady, a four-track, six-movement album. Avant-garde jazz: unconventional techniques and improvisation as the point.

Airfoils
